## 🧠 From question to decision

**Frame → Validate → Analyze → Explain**

- **Frame:** business context, stakeholder questions, requirements and success metrics.
- **Validate:** data quality, definitions, time periods and comparison groups.
- **Analyze:** patterns, segments, growth drivers and sensitivity to assumptions.
- **Explain:** readable dashboards, clear findings and recommendations tied to evidence.

<details>
<summary><b>📊 One finding that shows how I think</b></summary>

<br>

In my banking graduation project, successful transaction value grew **84.46%**. Average transaction value rose only **1.55%**, while successful transaction volume grew **81.64%**.

**The takeaway:** growth was primarily volume-led. That points toward investigating participation, repeat usage and reliable completion.

The comparison covers **1 January–29 June 2026 versus the same dates in 2025**. These are observed findings in an academic dataset, not results attributed to an implemented intervention.
